Logo

Meaning

The logo combines a wing within a hexagon. The hexagon evokes the silhouette of a cube and geometry, which represents creativity, 3D, and CGI. At its center, the wing symbolizes a passion for aviation and represents an eagle, a reference to my patron saint, St. John.

Design

Its design is intended to be elegant, sleek, sober, and streamlined. The clean, straight lines reflect my personal tastes and values.

Usage

This logo is primarily designed for use at larger sizes, such as a signature at the end of an animation video. Its thin strokes prevent it from looking heavy, but this elegance comes at the cost of legibility at very small sizes (e.g., as a browser favicon). It serves as a personal mark, a way to convey a piece of my identity through a simple, sober image rather than a corporate brand.

Typography

The primary typeface used across the site is Orkney, a geometric sans-serif font known for its clarity and modern feel.

Visual Motifs

Grain

A subtle layer of film grain is often applied to visuals to add texture, depth, and a touch of cinematic quality. It helps to unify different elements and avoid an overly digital or sterile look.

Blur

Selective blur and depth of field are used to guide the viewer's focus, create atmosphere, and enhance the sense of realism or stylization in CGI scenes. It is a key element in the site's artistic direction.
